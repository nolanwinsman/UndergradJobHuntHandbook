# Internships

This document is to explain a few things mostly unique to internships. The spark notes is you should try to get an internship. In a perfect world, more than one.

## What is the Point of Internships

I feel a lot of students don't fully understand why companies have internship programs.

- Is it because they want to exploit cheap labor?
- Is it charity to train up the next generation of Computer Scientists?
- Is it to make them do random grunt work FTEs don't want to do?
- Is it a tax loop hole?

If your answer was non of the above you would be correct. While there might be edge cases where some of the above options are true, for the most part the point of internships is to eventually hire the interns.

An internship is kinda like a three month test run to see if someone is a good fit at a company/position. It can be extremely difficult to fire people that just suck. You can't fire someone for being annoying or being a slight creep (to a degree) and slackers that do enough
work to not get fired but not enough to help their team.

A lot of people have seemed concerned about me recommending them for an internship at my company as they're scared if they do a bad job they'll make me look bad. This is missing the entire point of internships. If you show up and do an awful job, we just won't give you
a return offer and that's it. It's way better than giving you a full time offer then you suck.
